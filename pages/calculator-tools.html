Making informed financial decisions requires transparent mathematical formulas without hidden agendas. Whether calculating home loan installments, computing commercial GST tax liabilities, or determining exact chronological milestones, our calculators operate on proven financial algorithms:

Key Capabilities Across Our Calculator Suite:

  • EMI Loan Planning: Employs the standard reducing balance amortization method utilized by commercial banks worldwide. Visualizes principal vs interest allocation across the loan timeline.
  • Goods & Services Tax (GST): Provides exact intra-state (CGST + SGST) and inter-state (IGST) breakdowns with support for both tax-exclusive (cost + tax) and tax-inclusive (MRP back-calculation) workflows.
