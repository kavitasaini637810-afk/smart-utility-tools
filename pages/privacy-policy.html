Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Version 1.0

At Smart Utility Tools, protecting your personal privacy and data confidentiality is our primary engineering principle. This Privacy Policy discloses our data practices, detailing why and how your files and data are handled when utilizing our online tools.

1. Local Browser Processing (Zero-Upload Architecture)

Unlike conventional web services, our tools (including Word Counter, Percentage Calculator, Age Calculator, EMI Calculator, GST Calculator, Image Resizer, Image Compressor, PDF Merger, PDF Splitter, and PDF to Images) are engineered to execute entirely within your browser's client-side memory using standard HTML5 Canvas, JavaScript, and WebAssembly APIs.

When you upload an image, PDF file, or type confidential text:

  • No Server Upload: Your documents and input data are never transmitted over the internet to our web servers.
  • No Server Storage: We do not operate a backend file database, temporary file storage, or cloud bucket for uploaded materials.
  • Immediate Memory Release: When you refresh the webpage, navigate away, or close your browser tab, all temporary file data and object URLs are instantly cleared from your device's memory.

2. Data Collection and Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require user registration, usernames, passwords, or personal profile accounts.
  • We do not collect phone numbers, mailing addresses, financial credit card numbers, or bank account credentials.
  • We do not log, index, inspect, or retain the content of your PDF documents, processed images, or text statistics.

3. Third-Party Libraries and Resources

To deliver dependable PDF manipulation and ZIP file archiving, Smart Utility Tools utilizes stable, open-source JavaScript libraries (such as PDF-Lib, PDF.js, and JSZip). These libraries run strictly inside your local browser runtime and do not send your file contents to third-party endpoints.

4. Cookies and Advertising

Smart Utility Tools does not employ proprietary tracking cookies or invasive profiling mechanisms. If third-party services (such as Google AdSense for future non-intrusive advertising display, or standard static web hosting analytics) are configured by the website owner, those third parties may utilize standard cookies or web beacons according to their respective privacy terms.

5. Data Security Disclosure

While we have architected our tools to keep all computation confined to your local computer or smartphone, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely infallible. We encourage users to maintain up-to-date web browsers and secure personal device environments.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions, inquiries, or suggestions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: contact@example.com.
