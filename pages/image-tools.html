⚡ Best Practices for Image Optimization

Optimizing web images is crucial for fast page load times and mobile accessibility:

  • Choose the Right Dimensions: Never upload a 4000px raw camera photo to a website when it displays in a 600px container. Use the Image Resizer to downscale to exact requirements.
  • Select Next-Gen Formats: Modern formats like WebP offer superior compression efficiency compared to legacy JPEG or PNG without noticeable loss of visual clarity.
  • Adjust Quality Sensibly: A quality setting of 75%–85% typically reduces file size by 50% to 70% while remaining visually indistinguishable from the original to human eyes.
