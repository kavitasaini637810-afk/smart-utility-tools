    📖 How to Combine PDFs Securely

    1. Select Files: Drag and drop your PDF files into the drop area or click to select multiple files at once.
    2. Organize Sequence: Use the ▲ and ▼ buttons on each file row to arrange the documents in the exact sequence you want them merged.
    3. Delete Extras: Click the ✖ button to remove any accidental file selections.
    4. Merge & Download: Click Merge PDFs Now. Once compiled, click Download Merged PDF to save your combined document.

    💡 Practical Use Case

    Job Application Binder: Combine your Cover Letter (1 page), Resume (2 pages), and Recommendation Letters (3 pages) into one professional 6-page PDF document in seconds!
