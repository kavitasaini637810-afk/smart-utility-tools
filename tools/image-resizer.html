📖 How to Resize Images Safely

  1. Upload Image: Drag any photo or click the upload area.
  2. Adjust Dimensions: Enter your desired pixel width or height. With Maintain Aspect Ratio enabled, the other dimension automatically computes proportionally.
  3. Choose Format: Select PNG for graphics, JPEG for standard photos, or WebP for optimized web delivery.
  4. Download: Click Resize Image Now followed by Download Resized Image to save the newly dimensioned file.

💡 Practical Use Case

Website Banner: You take a 4032 × 3024 px photo with your phone camera (5.2 MB).
Using the resizer to scale it down to 1200 × 900 px reduces the file size to under 300 KB, boosting webpage loading speed significantly!
