📖 How to Resize Images Safely (इमेज का आकार बदलने का तरीका)

Resizing image pixel dimensions is one of the most effective ways to optimize website speed, meet job application requirements, and prepare photos for social media sharing. Follow these four simple steps:

  1. Select or Drag an Image: Drag any photo from your computer or phone into the upload box, or click the area to browse your file system. Supported formats include PNG, JPEG, JPG, WebP, and BMP.
  2. Configure Dimensions: Enter your target Width or Height in pixels. With Maintain Aspect Ratio enabled (recommended), our tool automatically calculates the matching dimension to prevent visual distortion or stretching.
  3. Choose Output Format: Select modern WebP for optimal web speed, JPEG for photos, or PNG when transparent layers or vector sharpness must be preserved.
  4. Process & Save: Click Resize Image Now to execute the canvas scaling, preview the dimension changes, and click Download Resized Image to save your file.
