📖 How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or Paste: Paste your written content directly into the text area above or start typing immediately.
  2. Live Computation: The statistics grid updates instantaneously with every keystroke.
  3. Unicode Support: Emojis, accented foreign characters, and multibyte symbols are accurately handled.
  4. Reading Time: Estimated based on standard average reading speeds of 200 words per minute.
  5. Action Buttons: Click Copy Text to quickly grab your updated text or Clear Text to reset the editor.

💡 Practical Example

Input Text: "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. It was a sunny morning!"
Live Output: 14 Words • 67 Characters (with spaces) • 54 Characters (without spaces) • 2 Sentences • 1 Paragraph • 5 sec Reading Time.
