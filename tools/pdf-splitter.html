📖 How Page Range Extraction Works (पेज अलग करने का तरीका)

Our PDF Splitter allows you to extract specific pages, continuous chapters, or custom ranges from any multi-page PDF document:

  • Single Page Extraction: Enter individual numbers separated by commas (e.g. 1, 3, 5) to extract specific non-consecutive pages.
  • Continuous Range Extraction: Use hyphens to define page ranges (e.g. 4-10) to extract an entire continuous section or chapter.
  • Combined Complex Queries: Combine both individual pages and ranges in one query (e.g. 1, 4-8, 12, 15-18).
  • Input Validation & Safety: The parser proactively checks that all requested page numbers exist within the uploaded document's page count and ensures start numbers never exceed end numbers.

📌 Range Syntax Guide with Examples

Syntax Pattern What It Extracts Ideal Scenario
1 Only Page 1 Extracting cover page or title summary sheet.
1-5 Pages 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 Extracting the executive summary or introduction chapter.
2, 4, 6, 8 Pages 2, 4, 6, and 8 Extracting alternate sheets or specific invoice schedules.
1-3, 7, 10-12 Pages 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 11, and 12 Extracting relevant contract agreements while omitting boilerplate pages.

💡 Practical Use Case

Real Estate Contract Scenario: You receive an 85-page residential lease PDF, but your accountant only needs the signed summary and payment schedule on pages 1, 2, and 83–85.
Enter: 1-2, 83-85 in the page range box. The tool extracts those 5 pages into a crisp, lightweight PDF document in under two seconds!
