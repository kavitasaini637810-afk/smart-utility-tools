PDF Splitter & Page Extractor

Extract single pages or custom ranges (such as 1-3, 5, 8-10) from any PDF document into a clean, new PDF file locally in your browser.

✂️
Select or Drag & Drop a PDF Document Here
Supports multi-page PDF documents
Use comma-separated single numbers or hyphenated ranges. Example: 1-3, 5, 8-10 extracts pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, and 10.

📖 How Page Range Extraction Works

  1. Upload Document: Drop any multi-page PDF file into the dropzone.
  2. Specify Ranges: Enter the 1-based page numbers you wish to isolate.
    • Single pages: 1, 4, 7
    • Continuous ranges: 2-5
    • Combined selections: 1-3, 5, 8-12
  3. Extract & Download: Click Extract Selected Pages followed by Download Extracted PDF to obtain your new document containing only the chosen pages.

💡 Practical Use Case

Tax Return Submission: Your accountant sends you a 25-page complete financial audit, but your bank only requests the summary schedule on pages 3 through 5 and page 12.
Enter: 3-5, 12 to create a lightweight 4-page PDF with just the requested records!
