Percentage Calculator

Solve everyday percentage problems instantly: find percentage values, determine proportions, and compute percentage increases or discounts.

📖 Understanding Percentage Formulas

  • Find X% of Y: Multiplies the total value $Y$ by the percentage divided by 100: $\text{Result} = (X / 100) \times Y$.
  • What % is X of Y: Compares part $X$ against whole $Y$: $\text{Percentage} = (X / Y) \times 100$. (Note: $Y$ cannot be zero).
  • Percentage Change: Computes the difference divided by the initial value: $\text{Change} = ((Y - X) / |X|) \times 100$.

💡 Practical Example

Shopping Discount Scenario: A jacket normally priced at $120 is on sale for $90.
Using Percentage Decrease (Original = 120, Discounted = 90):
$\text{Difference} = 120 - 90 = 30$. Formula: $(30 \div 120) \times 100 = \mathbf{25\% \text{ off}}$.
