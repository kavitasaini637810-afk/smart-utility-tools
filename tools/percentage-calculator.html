📖 Understanding Percentage Formulas (प्रतिशत निकालने के नियम)

Percentages are fundamental to financial planning, educational grading, commercial markups, and everyday shopping discounts. Our calculator provides four dedicated mathematical models:

  • 1. Calculate X% of Y (सीधा प्रतिशत): Finds the portion corresponding to a percentage of a given number.
    Formula: Result = (X ÷ 100) × Y
    Example: What is 15% of $80? $(15 div 100) imes 80 = mathbf{$12}$.
  • 2. What Percentage is X of Y? (हिस्सा प्रतिशत): Determines the proportional ratio of one number compared to another.
    Formula: Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100
    Example: You scored 45 marks out of 60 on a test. $(45 div 60) imes 100 = mathbf{75%}$.
  • 3. Percentage Increase / Growth (प्रतिशत वृद्धि): Measures the rate at which an initial value has grown to a higher number.
    Formula: Increase % = [(New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value] × 100
    Example: Stock price rose from $50 to $65. $[(65 - 50) div 50] imes 100 = mathbf{30% ext{ growth}}$.
  • 4. Percentage Decrease / Discount (प्रतिशत छूट / कमी): Measures the rate of reduction from an original higher number to a lower discounted number.
    Formula: Decrease % = [(Original Value - New Value) ÷ Original Value] × 100
    Example: A shirt originally priced at $120 is on sale for $90. $[(120 - 90) div 120] imes 100 = mathbf{25% ext{ discount}}$.

🛒 Everyday Real-World Applications

Percentage calculations are essential across personal finance, education, and business:

  • Retail Shopping & Seasonal Sales: Determine final sale prices and verify store discounts during clearance promotions.
  • Academic Marks & Examinations: Easily calculate overall percentages, GPA weights, and test scores for school, college, and competitive entrance exams.
  • Salary Appraisals & Annual Hikes: Verify your salary increment percentage by comparing previous compensation against revised compensation packages.
  • Business Margins & ROI: Analyze gross profit margins, return on investment (ROI), sales revenue quarterly growth, and supplier discount margins.

💡 Practical Calculation Example

Retail Discount Scenario: A jacket has an original price tag of $120, marked down to $90.
Using Percentage Decrease: Difference = $120 - $90 = $30. Formula: $(30 div 120) imes 100 = mathbf{25% ext{ off}}$. You save $30 (25%) on the purchase!
