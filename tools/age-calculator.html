📖 How Chronological Age is Calculated

Unlike basic day-counting approximations that divide total days by 365, our age calculator calculates exact calendar intervals:

  • Calendar Month Borrowing: If target day is earlier than the birth day, days are borrowed from the previous calendar month using the exact number of days in that specific month (28, 29, 30, or 31).
  • Leap-Year Awareness: Februaries in leap years (such as 2000, 2024, 2028) are counted as 29 days.
  • Timezone Normalization: Dates are parsed in your device's local timezone so day shift bugs do not alter your birthday.
