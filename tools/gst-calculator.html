📖 How GST Calculations Work

Goods and Services Tax can be computed in two standard directions:

  • GST Exclusive (Add GST): When you have the pre-tax base price:
    GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) ÷ 100
    Total Invoice Amount = Base Amount + GST Amount
  • GST Inclusive (Remove GST): When you have the gross price and need to determine how much was tax:
    Base Amount = Gross Amount ÷ (1 + (GST Rate ÷ 100))
    GST Amount = Gross Amount - Base Amount
  • Tax Distribution: For transactions within the same state (Intra-state), tax is split evenly between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For interstate transactions, Integrated GST (IGST) applies.

💡 Practical Calculation Example

Exclusive Example: Item price is $1,000 with 18% GST.
• GST Tax = $180 ($90 CGST + $90 SGST)
• Total Invoice = $1,180

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

IGST (Integrated GST) applies when goods or services are supplied across state borders or imported. CGST and SGST apply when the buyer and supplier reside within the same state.
