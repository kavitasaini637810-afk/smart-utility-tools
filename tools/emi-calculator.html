📖 Standard Reducing Balance EMI Formula

The standard Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) formula applied by commercial banks and financial lenders is:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1]

Where:
• P = Principal loan amount
• r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
• n = Loan duration in total number of monthly payments

If interest rate is 0%, the formula simplifies gracefully to: EMI = P / n.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

In a flat interest loan, interest is calculated on the full initial loan amount throughout the tenure. In reducing balance loans, interest is calculated each month only on the remaining unpaid principal, which significantly lowers your total interest burden.
Prepaying principal reduces your overall outstanding loan balance, allowing you either to shorten your loan tenure or request your lender to lower your future monthly EMI payments.
